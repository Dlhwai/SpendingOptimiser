# Notification-format research and local learning

## Current policy

Saved local samples now drive deterministic structural template discovery and reuse.
Independently public original messages and manual sample/template review are **not**
prerequisites for that local-code path. See [automatic transactions](automatic-transactions.md).
Application processing keeps private mail and intermediate templates out of AI context. Only closed,
safely reconstructed generic forms are shareable project assets; repeated/masked
source text is not presumed public.

This mechanism is not a universal bank parser. Each application must independently
resolve explicit financial semantics and account evidence. A learned pattern may
remain a candidate, with uncertain/unsupported records excluded rather than forced
into totals. Local qualification outputs and owner-specific paths are private task
material, not project documentation or published financial coverage claims.

## Earlier bounded public-reference investigation

The following public sources were checked before local-learning policy was clarified.
They remain useful context, not an activation gate or proof of an email grammar.

- DBS support: https://www.dbs.com.sg/personal/support/home.html and
  https://www.dbs.com.sg/personal/support/guide-card-security.html.
- DBS alert settings:
  https://www.dbs.com.sg/personal/support/bank-ibanking-notification-alerts.html.
  Describes delivery/preferences/thresholds, not a complete transaction-email grammar.
- https://www.dbs.com.sg/personal/support/card-security-transaction-alerts.html
  returned a spinner redirect during the investigation.
- Maybank Singapore requests to https://www.maybank2u.com.sg/en/personal/index.page,
  https://www.maybank2u.com.sg/en/personal/banking-services/index.page,
  https://www.maybank2u.com.sg/en/personal/banking-services/banking/transaction-alerts.page
  and https://www.maybank2u.com.sg/sitemap.xml timed out. Transport failure establishes
  nothing about the actual notification format.
- Trust FAQ pages at https://trustbank.sg/faqs/ and the credit-card, savings-account,
  pay-and-transfer and security subpages described email/push behavior and settings,
  not complete financial notification bodies. Their public frontend content endpoint
  was used, not private accounts.

No complete authoritative email example was obtained in that bounded search. Alert
availability, SMS/push examples and card terms are not themselves email-format
qualification. That historical result neither blocks local learning nor authorizes
missing financial facts, sender-based account inference or private-data disclosure.
